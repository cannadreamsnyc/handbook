01

ID & Age Check

Every customer needs a valid ID showing 21+ before they pass check-in. No exceptions, no matter how old someone looks.

  • State driver's license
  • State non-driver ID
  • Passport / passport card
  • Military ID
  • Permanent resident card

Reject if

Expired, damaged past checking a security feature, or doesn't match the person. If ID fails, decline entry politely and escalate to a shift lead if needed. Don't argue regulations on the floor.

02

On the Floor

  • Greet, then ask what they're looking for. No pitch first.
  • Speak to effects and category, never medical claims.
  • THC/CBD numbers come from the current label only, never memory.
  • Never serve anyone already visibly intoxicated.
  • Walk-ins are welcome, always.
03

Product Handling

  • Do not open a customer's order unless asked by management.
  • Damaged, expired, or recalled product comes off the floor immediately, flag it to a shift lead, not just set aside.
  • Every order is packed in a bag with the order receipt, plus the customer's name, number, and address.
04

Payment

In-store: cash, debit card, check transfer, or Aeropay.

Delivery is different

Cash is not allowed on delivery orders under NY OCM rules, delivery is prepaid only. Never tell a customer they can pay cash on a delivery order.

  • Count cash back out loud before closing the drawer.
  • Every sale gets a receipt.
  • Voids and refunds need shift lead approval, entered in the POS.

06

Zero Tolerance

  • No consumption on the premises, staff or customer. Cannadreams isn't licensed for it.
  • No personal use during a shift.
  • No discounting, comping, or gifting outside the POS, ever.
  • No taking product for personal use.
  • No sharing customer information outside store business.

Consequence

Any of the above is grounds for immediate suspension pending review, not a verbal warning.

07

Emergencies & Reporting

  • Robbery or threat: stay safe and notify the police as soon as possible.
  • Then notify the shift lead and owner.
  • Do not discuss any incident publicly or on social media.
  • See a coworker break a rule here, or get asked to? Report it to a shift lead or ownership directly. Good-faith reports aren't held against you.
